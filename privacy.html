Aimora Privacy Policy

Product: Aimora · Bundle ID: com.aimora.rangejournal

Aimora is a personal archery end-score journal that keeps practice records on your device. This Privacy Policy describes what Aimora can access, what stays local, and how purchases are handled.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Aimora iOS application used for logging archery ends, reviewing known scores, managing scoring rule versions, attaching optional target photos, and buying optional cosmetic gift packs.

Information stored on your device

  • End titles, rule names, distances, units, arrow scores (including miss zero and unknown arrows), notes, and timestamps
  • Rule versions you create, including allowed score sets and maximum arrows per end
  • Optional photos you capture or select for an end
  • Optional voice notes and speech-to-text drafts you create while editing an end
  • Local gift-balance and purchase history metadata for consumable in-app purchases

These items remain in on-device storage unless you export, share, clear local data, uninstall the app, or otherwise remove them through iOS system actions.

Permissions and when they are requested

  • Camera — requested only when you choose Take Photo for a target sheet or feedback image
  • Photo Library — requested only when you choose an existing photo or save an image
  • Microphone — requested only when you record a voice note
  • Speech Recognition — requested only when you convert speech into editable note text

Aimora does not request App Tracking Transparency. Aimora does not use a notification center delegate and does not schedule or send push or local notification content in this build.

Purchases

Optional gift packs are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment credentials and billing. Aimora stores only the local metadata needed to show gift balance and purchase history on this device. There is no restore purchases feature in this build because gift packs are treated as consumable cosmetic support items.

No account cloud for practice notes

Aimora does not require creating an account to record ends. Returning to the welcome screen logs you out of the local session. Clearing local data removes ends, purchases, and settings stored by Aimora on the device and returns you to the welcome screen.

Children

Aimora is intended for general audiences who manage their own practice records. Do not submit personal information of children through feedback photos or notes if you are not authorized to do so.

Contact

For privacy questions about Aimora, use the Support entry available from the App Store listing associated with this product.
